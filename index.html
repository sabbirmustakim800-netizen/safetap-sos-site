What it does

Built for the worst ten seconds of your day.

Every channel fires in parallel, and the ones that can't reach the network wait in a durable queue instead of disappearing.

⌁

Triple power-press

A foreground guard service watches screen on/off transitions and arms the alert on the third press inside 2.5 seconds.

⏱

5-second cancel window

Accidental press? Cancel from the overlay or the notification before anything is dialled or sent.

🔦

Siren, vibration, torch

The alert screen plus a siren loop and a blinking flashlight make you findable in the dark and the noise.

✆

Call then text

Dials 999, then sends an SMS with your safety card, coordinates and a Google Maps link to each contact.

⤾

Offline queue

No signal or no SMS permission? WorkManager keeps the alert and retries until it lands.

✚

Medical safety card

Blood group, allergies, medication and notes travel with every message so responders know what matters.

☁

Firebase backup

Optional Firestore backup of each alert under an anonymous account — never blocks the SMS flow.

◉

Ads where they belong

A single banner in Settings and History on the free plan. Never on the SOS button or the countdown.

The flow

From press to message in under five seconds.

  1. 01

    Arm

    Triple-press the power key, or tap and hold the orb on the home screen.

  2. 02

    Count down

    A five-second ring gives you one last chance to cancel.

  3. 03

    Fire every channel

    GPS fix (with last-known fallback) → WELL dial intent → siren and torch → SMS to contacts.

  4. 04

    Keep trying

    Failed sends queue locally and retry automatically; history shows each channel's status.

Install

No Android SDK required.

01

Download the APK

Tap the download button on this page, straight from your phone.

02

Allow this source

Android will ask to allow installs from your browser — accept once, then install.

03

Set up the card

Add blood group, allergies and 1–3 contacts, then grant SMS, call and location permissions.

Honest caveat: Android exposes no API for raw power-key presses, so SafeTap counts screen on/off transitions. Aggressive OEM battery managers (Xiaomi, Huawei, Oppo) should be told to exempt the app. The on-screen orb and the 999 quick-dial work on every device regardless.
