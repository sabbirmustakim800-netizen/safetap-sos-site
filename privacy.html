Privacy policy & disclaimer

Last updated: 26 November 2026 · Applies to the SafeTap SOS Android app and this website.

SafeTap is a helper tool, not an emergency service.

It is not connected to the police, ambulance or fire service. If you can dial, dial your local emergency number first (999 in Bangladesh, 911 in North America). Delivery depends on your phone, your mobile network, your permissions and your contacts' phones — a message can be delayed or fail.

What the app stores on your phone

  • Your safety card: name, blood group, allergies, conditions, medication and notes.
  • Up to three emergency contacts: name, phone number and relation.
  • Alert history with time, trigger, location and delivery status.
  • Messages that were prepared but not yet sent.

All of this lives in the app's private storage on the device. It is deleted when you use “Reset all local data” in Settings, or when you uninstall the app. Nothing is sold and nothing is used for advertising.

Location

Location is read only when an alert starts and while the alert screen is open. The coordinates are used to build a Google Maps link inside the alert text. The app does not track you in the background and keeps only the most recent fix.

Messages and calls

SafeTap does not send SMS and does not place calls on its own. It opens your messaging app or dialer with the recipient and the text already filled in, and you press send or call. Your mobile carrier and messaging app handle delivery. Blood group, allergies, conditions and medication are left out of the message unless you switch on “Include medical card in alert text” in Settings.

Cloud backup (optional, off by default)

If you enable Firebase backup, the alert record — time, trigger, location link and the list of channels — is written to a Google Cloud Firestore project under an anonymous account. You can turn it off at any time; turning it off stops new uploads but does not delete records already written. Email us to have them removed.

Ads

The free plan shows one AdMob banner in Settings and History. Ads are never shown on the SOS button, the countdown or the active-alert screen. Google may collect an advertising ID to serve ads. The paid plan removes all ad requests.

Payments

Paid plans are processed by NowPayments. The app never sees or stores card or wallet details; it only receives a payment status from our server. Our server stores the payment id and the plan it unlocks, not your wallet keys.

Permissions the app requests

  • Location — to include a map link in the alert.
  • Notifications — for the countdown and active-alert heads-up.
  • Foreground service — to keep triple power-press detection and the siren running.
  • Start after reboot — so the guard survives a restart.
  • Internet — only for optional cloud backup and ads.

SafeTap does not request the SMS or phone-call permissions.

Children and medical advice

SafeTap is not directed at children under 13 and is not a medical device. It gives no medical advice.

Contact

Questions, privacy requests or deletion requests: support@safetap.app. We answer within 30 days.
